# Fictional input: Motion to supplement the record and move the hearing

All names, rules, documents, and facts here are invented for training. Do not cite them as law or use them in a real filing.

## Assignment

Review Draft M-01 v1 and Proposed Order O-01 v1 in the fictional Lattice Harbor Review Tribunal. Use only the materials below. The underlying merits are out of scope.

## Supplied fictional rules

- **F-01 §2(a)**: After the exhibit cutoff, the movant must show reasonable diligence and that any concrete prejudice to the opponent can be cured by a specified measure. The tribunal may allow limited supplementation.
- **F-01 §2(b)**: The movant must show that moving a hearing is necessary, even after accounting for narrower scheduling adjustments. The tribunal may move a hearing independently of any decision on supplementation.
