# Motion Draft Reviewer: Review the order, not just the prose

## Abstract

A motion can read well while asking for relief that the record, the governing standard, or even the proposed order does not support. Motion Draft Reviewer is an instruction set for an AI-assisted internal review. It breaks a motion into requests for relief, tests each request against its applicable burden and record proof, and produces an ordered list of fixes. It is designed to help a lawyer decide what to verify, revise, narrow, or drop. It does not make that decision, assess filing authority, or submit anything.

## Why start with relief

Style review starts with sentences. This workflow starts with the words the judge is being asked to sign. A motion may use the same facts to support several requests, but those requests can turn on different standards and different procedural histories. If the brief asks to move one deadline and the order moves all deadlines, improving the argument misses the most consequential defect.

The reviewer therefore creates a separate entry for each operative request. Each entry records the precise action sought, the source of the court's authority, the movant's showing, and the party or proceeding affected. Any disagreement among the introduction, argument, prayer, and proposed order becomes a visible issue rather than an editing preference.

## Proof, provenance, and gaps

A useful review must enable a second lawyer to reproduce the finding. A bare reference to “Exhibit B” is not enough when the argument turns on the exact wording of one message. The workflow records the page, paragraph, or line and states what the source directly shows. If a conclusion requires a second inference, the inference appears separately. If a source contradicts the motion, the contradiction stays in the report. If no supporting item appears, the report says the support was absent from the supplied packet, not that it does not exist.

The same discipline applies to authority. An excerpt can support an initial read, but its scope and current status may need more investigation. The report distinguishes supplied, independently checked, and unchecked authorities. It asks the lawyer to verify the rule version, authority hierarchy, later history, and any relevant procedure before filing. An AI-generated quotation is not a substitute for the source.

## A fair test of the adverse position

A helpful reviewer identifies the adverse fact the drafter would least like to confront. It then separates three questions: What will the opponent say? What can the movant answer from this record? What additional evidence, process, or narrowing would make a difference? Where an opposition exists, the report distinguishes its actual position from other points the reviewer reconstructs. The review should never weaken or mischaracterize the opposing position to make the draft look stronger.

The fictional example demonstrates why a limited cure can be more defensible than a broad order. An internal message shows an exhibit was noticed but does not establish when it became available. The opponent shows an actual scheduling conflict. A candid review labels the diligence gap, proposes a bounded cure if the governing standard can be met, and separates that request from any need to change the hearing date.

## Deployment limits

The workflow does not need a hosted service or a connection to a docket. Provide the smallest useful record in an environment the firm has approved for the material. Exclude unrelated client information, confirm access and retention controls, and keep evidence originals intact. The tool does not create privilege, guarantee confidentiality, or replace the lawyer's responsibility for record accuracy, litigation judgment, and the final filing. Check the current rules and ethics guidance applicable to the engagement and jurisdiction.
